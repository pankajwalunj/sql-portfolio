## 📁 Projects

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis — MavenTech
> Analyzed 8,800 sales opportunities for a computer hardware company to uncover pipeline health, agent performance, and product trends.

**Business Questions Answered:**
- Which month had the highest deal volume?
- How long do Won vs Lost deals take to close?
- Who is the top performing sales agent by revenue?
- Which product series drives the most revenue?
- Which regional office leads in sales?

**Key Findings:**
- 48.2% win rate across all deals
- Darcel Schlecht generated $1,153,214 in revenue — top agent
- West region led all offices with $3,568,647
- GTX series dominates with $3,834,189 in revenue
- Won deals take 10 days longer to close than Lost — but worth pursuing

**Skills Used:** JOINs, GROUP BY, HAVING, Subqueries, CASE WHEN, DATEDIFF, Aggregations
